109 Edits Uploader

A small desktop tool used by one creator, the owner of the TikTok account @jaberedit.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

What it does

The creator makes short fan edits on their own computer. When an edit is finished, this tool sends the video to the creator's own TikTok account as a draft, through TikTok's official Content Posting API (upload to inbox).

Nothing is published by the tool. TikTok notifies the creator that a draft is waiting; the creator opens it in the TikTok app, reviews it, writes the caption and publishes it themselves.

Who can use it

Only the creator, on their own computer, with their own TikTok account. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts, sign-ups or uploads for anyone else.
