Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Summary

109 Edits Uploader runs on its owner's computer and talks only to TikTok. It collects nothing about anyone else, uses no analytics, sets no cookies and shares no data with third parties.

Permissions requested from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar), to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send the owner's finished videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts.

What is stored, and where

  • The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues after authorisation, and the account's open ID, are stored in a local file on the owner's computer only, so the tool can keep sending drafts.
  • Videos are read from the owner's computer and sent straight to TikTok's upload address. No copy is sent anywhere else.

How to remove access

In the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions → remove 109 Edits Uploader. The local token file can be deleted at any time.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used by one adult account owner.
